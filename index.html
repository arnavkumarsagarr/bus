Conductor holds cash notes in one hand and smartphone in the other. All core actions (station dropdowns, 1-touch fares, pax multiplier, 1-tap cash issue) sit within the 49% Thumb Comfort Arc with zero scrolling required.

Select destination and dispatch ticket for 3 passengers in ~1.3 seconds via Quick Pax chips ([1] [2] [3]) and 1-Tap Cash Issue button, completely surpassing the 4.0-second transit requirement.

Ergonomic Architecture Breakdown

  • Zero-Scroll Station Dropdowns: Native touch dropdowns consume only ~80px height, giving large touch surfaces during bus vibration and eliminating vertical page scrolling.
  • Draggable Handedness Puck: Floating controller flips between Left and Right hand thumb reach zones with a single horizontal thumb swipe.
  • Moving Bus Jolt Guard: 350ms input debouncing, 0.6s hold-to-confirm on cancellations (confined to phone frame), and synthesized tactile audio clicks.
  • Thermal Slip Simulation: Animated thermal paper slide-out receipt with dashed tear line (✂ - - - -) and vehicle serial tracking.
